## 🎯 Target Improvement Areas (Focus List)

1. **Question Word Order**:
   - ❌ _What it can do?_ $\rightarrow$ ✅ _What can it do?_
2. **Verb Patterns**:
   - `need + to + verb` (_need to read_)
   - `continue + -ing / to + verb` (_continue reading_)
   - `try + to + verb` (_try to learn_)
3. **Prepositions**:
   - _in the file_ (not _at the file_)
   - _based on_ (not _dựa vào_)
   - _answer to a question_ (not _answer of_)
4. **Word Suffixes**:
   - `-ation` (Noun), `-ity` (Noun), `-al` (Adjective).
