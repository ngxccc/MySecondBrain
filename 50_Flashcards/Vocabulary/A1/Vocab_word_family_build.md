What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **build**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /bɪld/
- **Meaning**: To construct, assemble, or create something by combining parts.
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `build` (/bɪld/ - xây dựng)
  - **Noun**: `builder` (/ˈbɪl.dər/ - người xây dựng) / `building` (/ˈbɪl.dɪŋ/ - tòa nhà / sự xây dựng)
- **Example**:
  - _Verb: We **build** scalable web applications._
  - _Noun: The software **builder** optimized the pipeline._
  - _Noun: The company moved to a new **building**._
