What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **use**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /juːz/
- **Meaning**: To employ or apply something for a specific purpose.
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `use` (/juːz/ - sử dụng)
  - **Noun**: `user` (/ˈjuː.zər/ - người dùng) / `use` (/juːs/ - việc sử dụng)
  - **Adjective**: `useful` (/ˈjuːs.fəl/ - hữu ích) / `useless` (/ˈjuːs.ləs/ - vô dụng)
- **Example**:
  - _Verb: We **use** Docker for containerization._
  - _Noun: The **user** authenticated via OAuth2._
  - _Adjective: Type annotations are **useful** for static analysis._
  - _Adjective: Dead code is **useless** and should be deleted._
