What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **learn**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /lɜːn/
- **Meaning**: To acquire knowledge or skill through study, experience, or teaching.
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `learn` (/lɜːn/ - học tập)
  - **Noun**: `learner` (/ˈlɜː.nər/ - người học) / `learning` (/ˈlɜː.nɪŋ/ - sự học tập)
- **Example**:
  - _Verb: Developers must **learn** new technologies continuously._
  - _Noun: An active **learner** practices Feynman presentations._
  - _Noun: Machine **learning** models analyze vast datasets._
