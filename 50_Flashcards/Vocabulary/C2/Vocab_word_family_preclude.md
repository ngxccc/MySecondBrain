What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **preclude**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /prɪˈkluːd/
- **Meaning**: To prevent something from happening or make it impossible.
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `preclude` (/prɪˈkluːd/ - ngăn ngừa, loại trừ)
  - **Noun**: `preclusion` (/prɪˈkluː.ʒən/ - sự ngăn ngừa)
- **Example**:
  - _Verb: Strict typing **precludes** runtime null pointer exceptions._
  - _Noun: The **preclusion** of memory leaks improves uptime._
