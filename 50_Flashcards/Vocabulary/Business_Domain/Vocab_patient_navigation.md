What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **patient navigation**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈpeɪ.ʃənt ˌnæv.ɪˈɡeɪ.ʃən/
- **Meaning**: Điều hướng người bệnh/khách hàng trên giao diện (Guiding users/patients smoothly through digital healthcare workflows and appointment booking.).
- **Example**:
  - _Streamlining patient navigation reduces bounce rate and improves booking rates._
