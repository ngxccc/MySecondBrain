What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **enquiry conversion**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ɪnˈkwaɪə.ri kənˈvɜː.ʃən/
- **Meaning**: Tỷ lệ chuyển đổi từ yêu cầu tư vấn (The rate or process of converting incoming client inquiries into active appointments.).
- **Example**:
  - _The primary goal is to improve appointment and enquiry conversion._
