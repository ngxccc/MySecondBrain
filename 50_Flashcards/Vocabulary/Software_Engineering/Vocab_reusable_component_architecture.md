What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **reusable component architecture**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /riːˈjuː.zə.bəl kəmˈpəʊ.nənt ˈɑː.kɪ.tek.tʃər/
- **Meaning**: Kiến trúc thành phần tái sử dụng (Structuring software around modular, self-contained UI or backend building blocks.).
- **Example**:
  - _Adopting a reusable component architecture accelerates feature delivery across teams._
