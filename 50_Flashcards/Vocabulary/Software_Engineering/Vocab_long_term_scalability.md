What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **long-term scalability**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌlɒŋˈtɜːm ˌskeɪ.ləˈbɪl.ə.ti/
- **Meaning**: Khả năng mở rộng lâu dài (The capacity of a software system to handle sustained traffic and feature growth over time.).
- **Example**:
  - _Designing for long-term scalability ensures sustainable platform growth._
