What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **slice literal**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Meaning**: A syntax construct used to declare and initialize a slice directly by listing elements inside curly braces `{}` without declaring an underlying array first or calling `make()`.
- **Example**:
  - _A **slice literal** allocates an anonymous underlying array and returns a slice header pointing to it._
