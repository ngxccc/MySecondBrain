What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **like-for-like migration**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌlaɪk.fəˈlaɪk maɪˈɡreɪ.ʃən/
- **Meaning**: Di chuyển hệ thống giữ nguyên cấu trúc/chức năng (Migrating data or applications to a new platform without altering functionality or layout.).
- **Example**:
  - _Executing a like-for-like migration minimizes unexpected regressions during launch._
