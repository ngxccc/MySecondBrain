What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **execute**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈek.sɪ.kjuːt/
- **Meaning**: To run, carry out, or perform an instruction or code (thực thi).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `execute` (/ˈek.sɪ.kjuːt/ - thực thi)
  - **Noun**: `execution` (/ˌek.sɪˈkjuː.ʃən/ - sự thực thi) / `executor` (/ɪɡˈzek.jə.tər/ - bộ thực thi)
  - **Adjective**: `executable` (/ˈek.sɪ.kjuː.tə.bəl/ - có thể thực thi)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `execute code` (thực thi mã nguồn)
  - `query execution` (sự thực thi truy vấn)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: The engine **executes** SQL queries efficiently._
  - _Noun: Fast query **execution** prevents database bottlenecks._
  - _Adjective: The compiled binary is fully **executable**._
