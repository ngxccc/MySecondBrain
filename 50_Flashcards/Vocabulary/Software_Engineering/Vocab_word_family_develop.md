What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **develop**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /dɪˈvel.əp/
- **Meaning**: To grow, build, or elaborate technical products or software systems.
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `develop` (/dɪˈvel.əp/ - phát triển)
  - **Noun**: `developer` (/dɪˈvel.ə.pər/ - nhà phát triển) / `development` (/dɪˈvel.əp.mənt/ - sự phát triển)
- **Example**:
  - _Verb: We **develop** modular monorail and microservice backends._
  - _Noun: A senior **developer** reviews pull requests thoroughly._
  - _Noun: Continuous integration speeds up **development**._
