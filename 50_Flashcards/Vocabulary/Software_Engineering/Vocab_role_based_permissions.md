What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **role-based permissions**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /rəʊl beɪst pəˈmɪʃ.ənz/
- **Meaning**: Phân quyền dựa trên vai trò (Access control mechanism restricting system capabilities based on assigned user roles (RBAC).).
- **Example**:
  - _Configuring role-based permissions prevents unauthorized access to patient data._
