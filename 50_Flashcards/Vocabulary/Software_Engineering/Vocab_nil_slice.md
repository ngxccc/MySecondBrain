What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **nil slice**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Meaning**: A slice with no allocated underlying array (`ptr = nil`, `len = 0`, `cap = 0`), which evaluates to `nil`.
- **Example**:
  - _A **nil slice** has no underlying memory allocated, whereas an empty slice literal `[]int{}` is initialized._
