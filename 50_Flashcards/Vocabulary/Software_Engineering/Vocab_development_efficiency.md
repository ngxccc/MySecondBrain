What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **development efficiency**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /dɪˈvel.əp.mənt ɪˈfɪʃ.ən.si/
- **Meaning**: Hiệu quả phát triển/lập trình (The rate and effectiveness with which engineering teams build and deliver features.).
- **Example**:
  - _Operating on a shared framework to improve governance and development efficiency._
