What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **leverage**?

---

- **Type**: Verb / Noun (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈliː.vər.ɪdʒ/
- **Meaning**: To use something to maximum advantage (tận dụng tối đa tài nguyên/công nghệ).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `leverage` (/ˈliː.vər.ɪdʒ/ - tận dụng tối đa)
  - **Noun**: `leverage` (/ˈliː.vər.ɪdʒ/ - đòn bẩy, lợi thế)
  - **Adjective**: `leveraged` (/ˈliː.vər.ɪdʒd/ - được tận dụng)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `leverage technology` (tận dụng công nghệ)
  - `leverage cache directives` (tận dụng các chỉ thị bộ nhớ đệm)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: The frontend **leveraged** Next.js 16 cache directives to reduce database query load._
  - _Noun: Cloudflare Tunnels provided security **leverage** without opening public inbound ports._
