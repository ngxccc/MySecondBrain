What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **gesticulation**?

---

- **Type**: Noun
- **Pronunciation**: /dʒesˌtɪk.jəˈleɪ.ʃən/
- **Meaning**: A gesture, especially a dramatic one, used instead of speaking or to emphasize one's words.
- **Example**:
  - _Effective **gesticulation** reinforces key points in technical explanations._
