What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **concurrency**?

---

- **Type**: Noun (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /kənˈkʌr.ən.si/
- **Meaning**: The ability of different parts or units of a program, algorithm, or problem to be executed out-of-order or in partial order, without affecting the outcome (tính đồng thời).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `concur` (/kənˈkɜːr/ - đồng ý / xảy ra đồng thời)
  - **Noun**: `concurrency` (/kənˈkʌr.ən.si/ - tính đồng thời)
  - **Adjective**: `concurrent` (/kənˈkʌr.ənt/ - đồng thời)
  - **Adverb**: `concurrently` (/kənˈkʌr.ənt.li/ - một cách đồng thời)
- **Collocations**:
  - `high concurrency` (mức độ đồng thời cao)
  - `concurrent checkouts` (các lượt thanh toán diễn ra đồng thời)
  - `concurrency control` (kiểm soát tương tranh)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Noun: Pessimistic locking prevented race conditions under high **concurrency**._
  - _Adjective: PostgreSQL handled thousands of **concurrent** read queries seamlessly._
  - _Adverb: Background workers executed the queue jobs **concurrently**._
