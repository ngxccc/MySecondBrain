What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **idempotency**?

---

- **Type**: Noun (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌaɪ.dəmˈpəʊ.tən.si/
- **Meaning**: The property of certain operations in mathematics and computer science whereby they can be applied multiple times without changing the result beyond the initial application (tính lũy thỏa).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `N/A`
  - **Noun**: `idempotence` / `idempotency` (/ˌaɪ.dəmˈpəʊ.tən.si/ - tính lũy thỏa)
  - **Adjective**: `idempotent` (/ˌaɪ.dəmˈpəʊ.tənt/ - có tính lũy thỏa)
  - **Adverb**: `idempotently` (/ˌaɪ.dəmˈpəʊ.tənt.li/ - một cách lũy thỏa)
- **Collocations**:
  - `idempotent API` (API có tính lũy thỏa)
  - `idempotency key` (khóa đảm bảo không lặp giao dịch)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Noun: Redis locks guaranteed webhook **idempotency** across concurrent payment callbacks._
  - _Adjective: The payment refund endpoint must be strictly **idempotent**._
  - _Adverb: The worker processed duplicated messages **idempotently**._
