What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **part of speech**?

---

- **Type**: Noun / Grammar Term
- **Pronunciation**: /pɑːt əv spiːtʃ/
- **Meaning**: A category of words (such as noun, verb, or adjective) that have similar grammatical properties.
- **Example**:
  - _Knowing the **part of speech** helps you place words correctly in a sentence._
