What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **compute**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /kəmˈpjuːt/
- **Meaning**: To calculate or process data using a computer or algorithm (tính toán, xử lý dữ liệu).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `compute` (/kəmˈpjuːt/ - tính toán)
  - **Noun**: `computation` (/ˌkɒm.pjəˈteɪ.ʃən/ - việc tính toán / sự xử lý)
  - **Adjective**: `computational` (/ˌkɒm.pjəˈteɪ.ʃən.əl/ - thuộc về tính toán)
  - **Adverb**: `computationally` (/ˌkɒm.pjəˈteɪ.ʃən.əl.i/ - bằng tính toán / xét về mặt tính toán)
- **Collocations**:
  - `compute models` (tính toán các mô hình)
  - `heavy computation` (việc tính toán phức tạp)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Offline batch jobs **compute** heavy ML models during off-peak hours._
  - _Noun: Hybrid **computation** balances server costs and real-time response._
  - _Adjective: Deep neural networks require high **computational** power._
  - _Adverb: Re-ranking millions of items in real-time is **computationally** expensive._
