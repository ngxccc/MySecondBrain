What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **summarize**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈsʌm.ə.raɪz/
- **Meaning**: To express the most important facts or ideas about something in a short form (tóm tắt).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `summarize` (/ˈsʌm.ə.raɪz/ - tóm tắt)
  - **Noun**: `summary` (/ˈsʌm.ər.i/ - bản tóm tắt) / `summarization` (/ˌsʌm.ər.aɪˈzeɪ.ʃən/ - sự tóm tắt)
  - **Adjective**: `summary` (/ˈsʌm.ər.i/ - ngắn gọn, tóm tắt)
  - **Adverb**: `summarily` (/səˈmer.əl.i/ - một cách tóm tắt)
- **Collocations**:
  - `concise summary` (bản tóm tắt ngắn gọn)
  - `summarize main points` (tóm tắt các điểm chính)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Could you teach me how to **summarize** the engineering article?_
  - _Noun: The student wrote a concise **summary** of Discord's architecture._
  - _Adjective: The report gives a **summary** view of system performance._
  - _Adverb: The results were **summarily** presented in the meeting._
