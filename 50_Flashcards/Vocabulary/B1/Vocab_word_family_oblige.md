What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **oblige**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /əˈblaɪdʒ/
- **Meaning**: To require or bind someone legally, morally, or logically to take action (bắt buộc, nghĩa vụ).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `oblige` (/əˈblaɪdʒ/ - bắt buộc)
  - **Noun**: `obligation` (/ˌɒb.lɪˈɡeɪ.ʃən/ - nghĩa vụ, bổn phận)
  - **Adjective**: `obligatory` (/əˈblɪɡ.ə.tər.i/ - bắt buộc)
  - **Adverb**: `obligatorily` (/əˌblɪɡ.əˈtɔːr.ə.li/ - một cách bắt buộc)
- **Collocations**:
  - `have an obligation to` (có nghĩa vụ phải làm gì)
  - `under no obligation` (không bị ràng buộc nghĩa vụ)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: The contract **obliges** us to deliver code on time._
  - _Noun: Engineers **have an obligation to** follow security standards._
  - _Adjective: Unit testing is **obligatory** before merging code._
  - _Adverb: Security audits are **obligatorily** performed annually._
