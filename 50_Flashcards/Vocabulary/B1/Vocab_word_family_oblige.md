What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **oblige**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /əˈblaɪdʒ/
- **Meaning**: To require or bind someone legally, morally, or logically to take action.
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `oblige` (/əˈblaɪdʒ/ - bắt buộc / làm ơn)
  - **Noun**: `obligation` (/ˌɒb.lɪˈɡeɪ.ʃən/ - nghĩa vụ, bổn phận)
  - **Adjective**: `obligatory` (/əˈblɪɡ.ə.tər.i/ - bắt buộc)
- **Key Collocations**:
  - **`have an obligation to do something`**: có nghĩa vụ / bổn phận phải làm gì
  - **`under an obligation`** / **`under no obligation`**: bị ràng buộc bởi nghĩa vụ / không chịu nghĩa vụ nào
- **Example**:
  - _Verb: The contract **obliges** us to deliver the code on time._
  - _Noun: Engineers **have an obligation to** follow security standards._
  - _Chunk: You are **under no obligation** to sign this agreement immediately._
  - _Adjective: Unit testing is **obligatory** before merging to main._
