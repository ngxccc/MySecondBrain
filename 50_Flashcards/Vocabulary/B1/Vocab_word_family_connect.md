What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **connect**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /kəˈnekt/
- **Meaning**: To join together or link two or more things.
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `connect` (/kəˈnekt/ - kết nối)
  - **Noun**: `connection` (/kəˈnek.ʃən/ - sự kết nối) / `connector` (/kəˈnek.tər/ - đầu nối / connector) / `connectivity` (/ˌkɒn.ekˈtɪv.ə.ti/ - tính kết nối)
- **Example**:
  - _Verb: The client **connects** to the WebSocket server._
  - _Noun: The database **connection** pool timed out._
  - _Noun: High **connectivity** enables real-time collaboration._
