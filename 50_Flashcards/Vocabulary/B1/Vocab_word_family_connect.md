What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **connect**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /kəˈnekt/
- **Meaning**: To join together or link two or more things (kết nối).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `connect` (/kəˈnekt/ - kết nối)
  - **Noun**: `connection` (/kəˈnek.ʃən/ - sự kết nối) / `connectivity` (/ˌkɒn.ekˈtɪv.ə.ti/ - tính kết nối)
  - **Adjective**: `connected` (/kəˈnek.tɪd/ - được kết nối)
  - **Adverb**: `connectively` (/kəˈnek.tɪv.li/ - theo cách kết nối)
- **Collocations**:
  - `database connection` (sự kết nối cơ sở dữ liệu)
  - `connect to server` (kết nối đến máy chủ)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: The client **connects** to the WebSocket server._
  - _Noun: The database **connection** pool timed out._
  - _Adjective: Ensure all microservices are properly **connected**._
  - _Adverb: Modules interact **connectively** via event buses._
