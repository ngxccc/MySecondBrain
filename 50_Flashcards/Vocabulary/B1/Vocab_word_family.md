What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **word family**?

---

- **Type**: Noun / Grammar Term
- **Pronunciation**: /wɜːd ˈfæm.əl.i/
- **Meaning**: A group of words that share a common base or root word with different suffixes.
- **Example**:
  - _Learning words in a **word family** makes it easy to master nouns, verbs, and adjectives._
