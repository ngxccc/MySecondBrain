What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **directional framework**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /daɪˈrek.ʃən.əl ˈfreɪm.wɜːk/
- **Meaning**: Khung định hướng/hướng dẫn mang tính gợi ý (A guiding set of structural principles rather than rigid rules.).
- **Example**:
  - _This information architecture is intended as a directional framework._
