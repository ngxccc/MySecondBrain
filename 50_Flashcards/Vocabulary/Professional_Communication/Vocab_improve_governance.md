What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **improve governance**?

---

- **Type**: Verb Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ɪmˈpruːv ˈɡʌv.ən.əns/
- **Meaning**: Cải thiện khả năng quản trị/kiểm soát (Enhancing structural rules, compliance, and oversight processes across systems.).
- **Example**:
  - _Operating on a shared framework helps improve governance across multi-brand sites._
