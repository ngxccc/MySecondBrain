What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **automated**?

---

- **Type**: Adjective
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈɔː.tə.meɪ.tɪd/
- **Meaning**: Operated or executed automatically by software scripts or machinery.
- **Example**:
  - _run automated tests_
