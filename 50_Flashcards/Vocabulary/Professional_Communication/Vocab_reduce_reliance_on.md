What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **reduce reliance on**?

---

- **Type**: Verb Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /rɪˈdjuːs rɪˈlaɪ.əns ɒn/
- **Meaning**: Giảm sự phụ thuộc vào (Decreasing dependency on external libraries, legacy tools, or manual operational steps.).
- **Example**:
  - _Automating CI/CD pipelines helps reduce reliance on manual deployments._
