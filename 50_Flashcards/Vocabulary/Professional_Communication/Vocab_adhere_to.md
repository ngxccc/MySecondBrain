What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **adhere to**?

---

- **Type**: Verb Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ədˈhɪər tuː/
- **Meaning**: To follow or comply with a rule, standard, or practice.
- **Example**:
  - _consistently adhere to regulations_
