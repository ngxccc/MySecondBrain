What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **solve**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /sɒlv/
- **Meaning**: To find an answer to or explanation for a problem or obstacle.
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `solve` (/sɒlv/ - giải quyết)
  - **Noun**: `solution` (/səˈluː.ʃən/ - giải pháp) / `solver` (/ˈsɒl.vər/ - bộ giải)
- **Example**:
  - _Verb: First Principles Thinking helps **solve** complex bugs._
  - _Noun: Redis cache provides an optimal **solution** for high throughput._
  - _Noun: The constraint **solver** verified the schema._
