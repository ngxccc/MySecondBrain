What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **manage**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈmæn.ɪdʒ/
- **Meaning**: To be in charge of, control, or direct operations and resources.
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `manage` (/ˈmæn.ɪdʒ/ - quản lý)
  - **Noun**: `manager` (/ˈmæn.ɪ.dʒər/ - người quản lý) / `management` (/ˈmæn.ɪdʒ.mənt/ - sự quản lý)
- **Example**:
  - _Verb: The orchestrator **manages** container instances._
  - _Noun: The engineering **manager** conducts weekly reviews._
  - _Noun: State **management** is essential in React applications._
