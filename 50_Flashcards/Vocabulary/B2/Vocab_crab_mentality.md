What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **crab mentality**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /kræb menˈtæl.ə.ti/
- **Meaning**: A mindset where members of a group try to undermine or pull down anyone who achieves success or tries to improve themselves ("if I can't have it, neither can you").
- **Example**:
  - _Mocking someone for improving their English pronunciation is a classic sign of **crab mentality**._
