What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **Uncanny Valley Effect**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ʌnˈkæn.i ˈvæl.i ɪˈfekt/
- **Meaning**: A phenomenon where something that looks or sounds almost natural—but slightly off—causes feelings of discomfort or unnaturalness.
- **Example**:
  - _Exaggerating an artificial American accent creates an **Uncanny Valley Effect** in speech perception._
