What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **expand**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ɪkˈspænd/
- **Meaning**: To become or make larger or more extensive in size, volume, or quantity (mở rộng, tăng trưởng).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `expand` (/ɪkˈspænd/ - mở rộng, tăng trưởng)
  - **Noun**: `expansion` (/ɪkˈspæn.ʃən/ - sự mở rộng)
  - **Adjective**: `expansive` (/ɪkˈspæn.sɪv/ - có xu hướng mở rộng) / `expandable` (/ɪkˈspæn.də.bəl/)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `expand rapidly` (mở rộng nhanh chóng)
  - `subscriber expansion` (sự tăng trưởng người dùng)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Netflix's subscriber base **expanded** from 260 million in 2023 to over 325 million in 2026._
  - _Noun: The rapid **expansion** of mobile streaming boosted Asia-Pacific memberships._
  - _Adjective: The company maintains an **expansive** infrastructure across global data centers._
