What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **implicit**?

---

- **Type**: Adjective (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ɪmˈplɪs.ɪt/
- **Meaning**: Suggested though not directly expressed; automatically derived from behavior (ngầm, ẩn).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `imply` (/ɪmˈplaɪ/ - ngầm chỉ, hàm ý)
  - **Noun**: `implication` (/ˌɪm.plɪˈkeɪ.ʃən/ - sự hàm ý / sự liên quan)
  - **Adjective**: `implicit` (/ɪmˈplɪs.ɪt/ - ngầm, không trực tiếp)
  - **Adverb**: `implicitly` (/ɪmˈplɪs.ɪt.li/ - một cách ngầm hiểu)
- **Collocations**:
  - `implicit data` (dữ liệu ngầm/ẩn)
  - `implicit feedback` (phản hồi gián tiếp)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Rewatching a movie scene **implies** strong user preference._
  - _Noun: What are the architecture **implications** of logging implicit data?_
  - _Adjective: Watch history and dwell time are examples of **implicit** data._
  - _Adverb: The system **implicitly** captures browsing patterns without user input._
