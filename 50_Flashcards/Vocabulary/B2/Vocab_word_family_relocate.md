What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **relocate**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌriː.ləʊˈkeɪt/
- **Meaning**: To move or transfer something to a new place or position in a document or system (di chuyển / dời sang vị trí mới).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `relocate` (/ˌriː.ləʊˈkeɪt/ - di chuyển vị trí)
  - **Noun**: `relocation` (/ˌriː.ləʊˈkeɪ.ʃən/ - sự di chuyển vị trí)
  - **Adjective**: `relocated` (/ˌriː.ləʊˈkeɪ.tɪd/)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `relocate statistics` (di chuyển số liệu sang vị trí mới)
  - `relocate content` (di chuyển nội dung)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: We **relocated** the subscriber count metric from Slide 1 to Slide 9._
  - _Noun: Content **relocation** improved the logical narrative flow of the speech._
  - _Adjective: The **relocated** stat now aligns perfectly with the visual card on Slide 9._
