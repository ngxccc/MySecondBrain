What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **evaluate**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ɪˈvæl.ju.eɪt/
- **Meaning**: To judge or calculate the quality, importance, amount, or value of something (đánh giá).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `evaluate` (/ɪˈvæl.ju.eɪt/ - đánh giá)
  - **Noun**: `evaluation` (/ɪˌvæl.juˈeɪ.ʃən/ - sự đánh giá) / `evaluator` (/ɪˈvæl.ju.eɪ.tər/ - người đánh giá)
  - **Adjective**: `evaluative` (/ɪˈvæl.ju.ə.tɪv/ - mang tính đánh giá)
  - **Adverb**: `evaluatively` (/ɪˈvæl.ju.ə.tɪv.li/ - một cách đánh giá)
- **Collocations**:
  - `critical evaluation` (sự đánh giá mang tính phản biện)
  - `evaluate performance` (đánh giá hiệu năng)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Should I **evaluate** the full article before writing the report?_
  - _Noun: The team conducted a critical **evaluation** of Discord's architecture._
  - _Adjective: The paper offers an **evaluative** perspective on memory management._
  - _Adverb: The benchmark metrics were **evaluatively** analyzed in the report._
