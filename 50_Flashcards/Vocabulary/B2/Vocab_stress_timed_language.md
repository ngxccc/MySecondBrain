What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **stress-timed language**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /stres taɪmd ˈlæŋ.ɡwɪdʒ/
- **Meaning**: A language (like English) where the duration between stressed syllables is equal, regardless of how many unstressed syllables fall between them.
- **Example**:
  - _English is a **stress-timed language**, which means rhythm relies on stress rather than syllable count._
