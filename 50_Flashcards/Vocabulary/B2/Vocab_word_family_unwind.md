What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **unwind**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ʌnˈwaɪnd/
- **Meaning**: To relax after a period of work or tension, or to uncoil (thư giãn, xả stress sau giờ làm việc căng thẳng).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `unwind` (/ʌnˈwaɪnd/ - thư giãn, xả stress)
  - **Noun**: `unwinding` (/ʌnˈwaɪn.dɪŋ/ - sự thư giãn)
  - **Adjective**: `unwound` (/ʌnˈwaʊnd/ - đã được tháo gỡ/thư giãn)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `unwind after work` (xả stress sau giờ làm)
  - `a way to unwind` (một cách để thư giãn)
  - `unwind with a game` (thư giãn bằng một trò chơi)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Playing rhythm games helps me **unwind** after a long day of coding._
  - _Noun: Watching a movie provides a peaceful **unwinding** period before sleep._
  - _Verb (Past): He **unwound** by listening to electronic music and playing Just Shapes & Beats._
