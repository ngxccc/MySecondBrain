What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **analogy**?

---

- **Type**: Noun (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /əˈnæl.ə.dʒi/
- **Meaning**: A comparison between two things to help explain an idea or concept (phép ẩn dụ / sự tương tự).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `analogize` (/əˈnæl.ə.dʒaɪz/ - so sánh tương tự)
  - **Noun**: `analogy` (/əˈnæl.ə.dʒi/ - phép tương tự / ẩn dụ)
  - **Adjective**: `analogous` (/əˈnæl.ə.ɡəs/ - tương tự)
  - **Adverb**: `analogously` (/əˈnæl.ə.ɡəs.li/ - một cách tương tự)
- **Collocations**:
  - `draw an analogy` (rút ra sự tương tự)
  - `analogous to` (tương tự với)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: We can **analogize** Garbage Collection to stopping a car for maintenance._
  - _Noun: The tutor used a driving **analogy** to explain latency spikes._
  - _Adjective: The GC pause in Go is **analogous** to a sudden brake._
  - _Adverb: The two algorithms perform **analogously** under high concurrency._
