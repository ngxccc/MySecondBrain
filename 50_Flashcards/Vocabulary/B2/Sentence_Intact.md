What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **Intact**?

---

- **Type**: Adjective
- **Pronunciation**: /ɪnˈtækt/
- **Meaning**: Not damaged or impaired; remaining whole and complete.
- **Example**:
  - _Your learner profile remains **intact** after clear._
