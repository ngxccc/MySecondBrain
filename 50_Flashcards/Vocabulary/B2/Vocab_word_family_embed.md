What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **embed**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ɪmˈbed/
- **Meaning**: To fix or insert an object, video, or code firmly into surrounding material or a slide (nhúng / gắn vào).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `embed` (/ɪmˈbed/ - nhúng, gắn vào)
  - **Noun**: `embedment` (/ɪmˈbed.mənt/) / `embedding` (/ɪmˈbed.ɪŋ/ - sự nhúng / véc-tơ nhúng)
  - **Adjective**: `embedded` (/ɪmˈbed.ɪd/ - được nhúng)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `embed a video` (nhúng một video)
  - `embedded media` (truyền thông được nhúng)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Can I **embed** a short UI video into the slide?_
  - _Noun: Video **embedment** enhances visual presentation quality._
  - _Adjective: The **embedded** GIF plays automatically during the talk._
