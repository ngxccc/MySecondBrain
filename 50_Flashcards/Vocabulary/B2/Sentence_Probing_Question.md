What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **Probing Question**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈprəʊ.bɪŋ ˈkwes.tʃən/
- **Meaning**: A question designed to uncover deeper information or underlying truth.
- **Example**:
  - _The skill asks **probing questions** about edge cases and performance._
