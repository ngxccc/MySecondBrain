What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **First Principles**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌfɜːst ˈprɪn.sə.pəlz/
- **Meaning**: Basic foundational propositions or assumptions that cannot be deduced any further.
- **Example**:
  - _We apply **first principles** thinking to learn English._
