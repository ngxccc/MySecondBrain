What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **On Auto Pilot**?

---

- **Type**: Prepositional Phrase / Idiom
- **Pronunciation**: /ɒn ˈɔː.təʊ ˈpaɪ.lət/
- **Meaning**: Doing something routinely or automatically without conscious thought.
- **Example**:
  - _Creating this interview process prevents us from working **on auto-pilot**._
