What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **Streamline**?

---

- **Type**: Verb
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈstriːm.laɪn/
- **Meaning**: To make an organization, process, or system more efficient and effective.
- **Example**:
  - _We **streamline** our study process using AI._
