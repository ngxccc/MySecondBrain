What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the verb **over-pronounce**?

---

- **Type**: Verb
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌəʊ.və.prəˈnaʊns/
- **Meaning**: To pronounce words with excessive force, unnatural tension, or theatrical exaggeration.
- **Example**:
  - _Learners often **over-pronounce** individual syllables instead of focusing on natural connected speech._
