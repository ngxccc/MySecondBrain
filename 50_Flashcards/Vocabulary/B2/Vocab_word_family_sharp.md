What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **sharp**?

---

- **Type**: Adjective (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ʃɑːrp/
- **Meaning**: Producing a sudden, clear, or steep change; distinct or acute (sắc, dốc đứng, rõ rệt).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `sharpen` (/ˈʃɑː.pən/ - làm sắc, mài giũa)
  - **Noun**: `sharpness` (/ˈʃɑːp.nəs/ - độ sắc nét / sự dốc đứng)
  - **Adjective**: `sharp` (/ʃɑːrp/ - dốc đứng, rõ rệt)
  - **Adverb**: `sharply` (/ˈʃɑːp.li/ - một cách đột ngột)
- **Collocations**:
  - `sharp increase` (sự tăng dốc đứng)
  - `rise sharply` (tăng lên đột ngột)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Technical reports will **sharpen** your analytical skills._
  - _Noun: The **sharpness** of the curve indicated a sudden GC pause._
  - _Adjective: The latency chart displayed a **sharp** peak._
  - _Adverb: Latency rose **sharply** from 10ms to 2000ms._
