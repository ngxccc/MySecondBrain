What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **Cognitive Fatigue**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈkɒɡ.nə.tɪv fəˈtiːɡ/
- **Meaning**: Mental exhaustion caused by prolonged cognitive activity.
- **Example**:
  - _Prompting in English causes **cognitive fatigue**._
