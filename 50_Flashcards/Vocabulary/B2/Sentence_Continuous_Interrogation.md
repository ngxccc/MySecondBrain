What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **Continuous Interrogation**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /kənˈtɪn.ju.əs ɪnˌter.əˈɡeɪ.ʃən/
- **Meaning**: Ongoing rigorous questioning and verification of assumptions.
- **Example**:
  - _**Continuous interrogation** helps uncover hidden assumptions in complex code._
