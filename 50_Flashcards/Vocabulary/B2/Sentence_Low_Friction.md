What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **Low Friction**?

---

- **Type**: Adjective
- **Pronunciation**: /ləʊ ˈfrɪk.ʃən/
- **Meaning**: Involving minimal difficulty, resistance, or effort.
- **Example**:
  - _We need a **low-friction** learning method._
