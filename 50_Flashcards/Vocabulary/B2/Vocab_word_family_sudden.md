What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **sudden**?

---

- **Type**: Adjective (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈsʌd.ən/
- **Meaning**: Happening or done quickly and unexpectedly (đột ngột, bất ngờ).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `N/A`
  - **Noun**: `suddenness` (/ˈsʌd.ən.nəs/ - sự đột ngột)
  - **Adjective**: `sudden` (/ˈsʌd.ən/ - đột ngột)
  - **Adverb**: `suddenly` (/ˈsʌd.ən.li/ - một cách đột ngột)
- **Collocations**:
  - `sudden spike` (sự tăng vọt đột ngột)
  - `sudden change` (sự thay đổi đột ngột)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Noun: The **suddenness** of the outage caught engineers off guard._
  - _Adjective: There was a **sudden** spike in latency every two minutes._
  - _Adverb: The Go service **suddenly** slowed down due to memory allocation._
