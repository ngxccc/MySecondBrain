What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **Coding Blindly**?

---

- **Type**: Verb Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈkəʊ.dɪŋ ˈblaɪnd.li/
- **Meaning**: Writing code without understanding the underlying mechanics or consequences.
- **Example**:
  - _We need an interrogation skill to prevent developers from **coding blindly**._
