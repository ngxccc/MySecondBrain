What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **voiced sound**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Meaning**: A sound produced with vocal cord vibration, including all vowels and consonants like /b/, /d/, /g/, /v/, /z/, /m/, /n/, or /l/.
- **Example**:
  - _Touch your neck to feel if the vocal cords vibrate when making a **voiced sound**._
