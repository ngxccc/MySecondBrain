What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **horribly**?

---

- **Type**: Adverb
- **Meaning**: Extremely or terribly (used as an intensifier for negative states or low efficiency).
- **Example**:
  - _Copying an entire 10,000-element array on every function call would be **horribly** slow._
