What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **incorporate**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ɪnˈkɔː.pər.eɪt/
- **Meaning**: To include or combine something as a part of a whole system or document (tích hợp / kết hợp).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `incorporate` (/ɪnˈkɔː.pər.eɪt/ - tích hợp, kết hợp)
  - **Noun**: `incorporation` (/ɪnˌkɔː.pərˈeɪ.ʃən/ - sự tích hợp)
  - **Adjective**: `incorporated` (/ɪnˈkɔː.pər.eɪ.tɪd/ - được tích hợp)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `incorporate feedback` (tích hợp phản hồi)
  - `seamlessly incorporate` (tích hợp một cách mượt mà)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: We **incorporated** timestamp footnotes and 2026 data updates into the script._
  - _Noun: The **incorporation** of real-time device context improves homepage ranking._
  - _Adjective: The **incorporated** changes reflect official Netflix research papers._
