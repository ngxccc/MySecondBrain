What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **First Principles Thinking**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌfɜːst ˈprɪn.sə.pəlz ˈθɪŋ.kɪŋ/
- **Meaning**: A problem-solving technique of breaking down a complex problem into its most basic, foundational truths and reasoning up from there.
- **Example**:
  - _We applied **First Principles Thinking** to understand why exaggerated accents trigger social judgment._
