What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **substitute**?

---

- **Type**: Verb / Noun (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈsʌb.stɪ.tʃuːt/
- **Meaning**: To use something or someone in place of another; a replacement (thay thế, vật/người thay thế).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `substitute` (/ˈsʌb.stɪ.tʃuːt/ - thay thế: substitute A for B)
  - **Noun**: `substitution` (/ˌsʌb.stɪˈtʃuː.ʃən/ - sự thay thế) / `substitute` (/ˈsʌb.stɪ.tʃuːt/ - vật/người thay thế)
  - **Adjective**: `substitutable` (/ˌsʌb.stɪˈtjuː.tə.bəl/ - có thể thay thế được) / `substitute` (/ˈsʌb.stɪ.tʃuːt/ - mang tính thay thế, dự bị)
  - **Adverb**: `substitutively` (/ˈsʌb.stɪ.tʃuː.tɪv.li/ - theo cách thay thế)
- **Collocations**:
  - `substitute A for B` (dùng A thay thế cho B)
  - `direct substitution` (sự thay thế trực tiếp)
  - `drop-in substitute` (vật thay thế tương thích trực tiếp)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: We can **substitute** Bun for Node.js to accelerate test execution._
  - _Noun: Bun serves as a high-performance **substitute** for Node.js in modern workflows._
  - _Noun: The **substitution** of legacy services with Go handlers reduced CPU usage._
  - _Adjective: The two runtime interfaces are completely **substitutable** in our test suite._
