What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **monolithic**?

---

- **Type**: Adjective (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌmɒn.əˈlɪθ.ɪk/
- **Meaning**: Formed of a single large block of stone; in software engineering: massive, uniform, indivisible, and lacking modular structure (nguyên khối, đơn khối đồ sộ khó chia tách).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `N/A`
  - **Noun**: `monolith` (/ˈmɒn.ə.lɪθ/ - khối đơn nhất, kiến trúc nguyên khối)
  - **Adjective**: `monolithic` (/ˌmɒn.əˈlɪθ.ɪk/ - mang tính nguyên khối)
  - **Adverb**: `monolithically` (/ˌmɒn.əˈlɪθ.ɪ.kəl.i/ - theo kiểu nguyên khối)
- **Collocations**:
  - `monolithic file` (file dữ liệu nguyên khối quá lớn)
  - `monolithic architecture` (kiến trúc nguyên khối)
  - `modular monolith` (kiến trúc nguyên khối nhưng được module hóa chặt chẽ)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Adjective: Storing all error histories in a single **monolithic** JSON file creates high editing friction._
  - _Noun: The engineering team refactored the legacy backend **monolith** into distinct domain packages._
  - _Adverb: The application was deployed **monolithically** as a single container binary._
