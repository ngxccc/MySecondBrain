What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **redundant**?

---

- **Type**: Adjective (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /rɪˈdʌn.dənt/
- **Meaning**: Superfluous, unnecessary, or repetitive in a text, script, or system (dư thừa, trùng lặp).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `N/A`
  - **Noun**: `redundancy` (/rɪˈdʌn.dən.si/ - sự dư thừa, sự trùng lặp)
  - **Adjective**: `redundant` (/rɪˈdʌn.dənt/ - dư thừa, trùng lặp)
  - **Adverb**: `redundantly` (/rɪˈdʌn.dənt.li/ - một cách dư thừa)
- **Collocations**:
  - `redundant information` (thông tin dư thừa)
  - `eliminate redundancy` (loại bỏ sự trùng lặp)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Noun: We refined Slide 9 to eliminate script **redundancy** regarding subscriber counts._
  - _Adjective: Repeating the subscriber total on both Slide 1 and Slide 9 felt **redundant**._
  - _Adverb: Avoid stating identical metrics **redundantly** across different presentation sections._
