What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **satisfy**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈsæt.ɪs.faɪ/
- **Meaning**: To fulfill a need, desire, or expectation; to make content (làm hài lòng / thỏa mãn).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `satisfy` (/ˈsæt.ɪs.faɪ/ - làm hài lòng)
  - **Noun**: `satisfaction` (/ˌsæt.ɪsˈfæk.ʃən/ - sự hài lòng)
  - **Adjective**: `satisfactory` (/ˌsæt.ɪsˈfæk.tər.i/ - thỏa đáng) / `satisfied` (/ˈsæt.ɪs.faɪd/ - hài lòng)
  - **Adverb**: `satisfactorily` (/ˌsæt.ɪsˈfæk.tər.əl.i/ - một cách thỏa đáng)
- **Collocations**:
  - `user satisfaction` (sự hài lòng của người dùng)
  - `satisfy requirements` (thỏa mãn các yêu cầu)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Personalized recommendations **satisfy** individual user tastes._
  - _Noun: Rapid content discovery boosts overall **satisfaction**._
  - _Adjective: The system delivered a **satisfactory** recommendation response._
  - _Adverb: The pipeline functions **satisfactorily** during peak hours._
