What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **flaw**?

---

- **Type**: Noun / Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /flɔː/
- **Meaning**: A fault, mistake, or weakness in a system, argument, or design (khiếm khuyết, lỗi).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `flaw` (/flɔː/ - làm hỏng, tạo khiếm khuyết)
  - **Noun**: `flaw` (/flɔː/ - khiếm khuyết, lỗi)
  - **Adjective**: `flawless` (/ˈflɔː.ləs/ - hoàn hảo, không tì vết) / `flawed` (/flɔːd/ - có khiếm khuyết)
  - **Adverb**: `flawlessly` (/ˈflɔː.ləs.li/ - một cách hoàn hảo)
- **Collocations**:
  - `flawless execution` (sự thực thi hoàn hảo)
  - `architectural flaw` (khiếm khuyết kiến trúc)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Race conditions **flaw** multi-threaded data pipelines._
  - _Noun: The technical audit revealed zero architectural **flaws** in the slide deck._
  - _Adjective: The presentation delivered a **flawless** explanation of Nearline processing._
  - _Adverb: The recommendation pipeline executed **flawlessly** during peak hours._
