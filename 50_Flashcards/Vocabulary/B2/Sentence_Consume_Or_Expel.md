What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **Consume Or Expel**?

---

- **Type**: Verb Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /kənˈsjuːm ɔːr ɪkˈspel/
- **Meaning**: To absorb or eject energy, resources, or data.
- **Example**:
  - _You can bind **consume-or-expel-window-left** to move a single window into the column on its left._
