What is the correct subject-verb agreement for **You** in past tense (You were / You didn't)?

---

- **Formula / Pattern**:
  - `To Be (Past)`: `You were...` / `You were not (weren't)...`
  - `Action Verb (Past)`: `You didn't + V-bare`
- **Core Explanation**:
  - Với đại từ `You` (ngôi thứ 2), động từ `to be` ở quá khứ LUÔN dùng `were` (không dùng `was`). Phủ định hành động dùng `didn't + V-bare`.
- **Usage & Anchor Cues**:
  - ❌ `You was...` / ❌ `You was not activate...`
  - ✅ `You were...` / ✅ `You didn't activate...`
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _`You were not in the office yesterday.`_ (Bạn đã không ở văn phòng hôm qua.)
  - _`You didn't activate the feature flag.`_ (Bạn đã không kích hoạt feature flag.)
